**Regression Quantification & Analysis**

**Summary:**
The statements page performance degraded significantly after release 4.7.0, with `GetStatementLineQuery` requests taking 35–73 seconds (vs. 280–320ms pre-release), and thread pool starvation detected. The `GetAccountSummaryQuery` remained relatively stable (~3–4s), suggesting the regression is tied to the statement query, not a general slowdown.

---

**Current State:**
- **Status:** Open, Unresolved, High Priority
- **Assignee:** Tom Becker (initially, but comment suggests he’s skeptical)
- **Blocking:** No known fix version or sprint; depends on root cause identification.

---

**Key Details:**

- **Pre-Release (4.6.3):**
  - `GetStatementLineQuery`: 281–312ms (avg ~300ms)
  - `GetAccountSummaryQuery`: 3102–3255ms (avg ~3200ms)
  - No long-running requests or thread pool starvation.

- **Post-Release (4.7.0):**
  - `GetStatementLineQuery`: 35171ms–72974ms (avg ~50,000ms), with 4+ instances exceeding 10s threshold.
  - `GetAccountSummaryQuery`: 3188ms–3402ms (avg ~3250ms) — unchanged.
  - Thread pool starvation detected: 31 queued items, 0 threads available for 4200ms.
  - No health check failures.

- **Trigger:** Release 4.7.0 included a migration: `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`. This likely caused the query to reindex or recompute large datasets, leading to performance degradation.
