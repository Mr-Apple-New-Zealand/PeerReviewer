### **Root Cause**
- **JWT signing key rotation** in config deploy **OPS-81** at 09:30.
- The **Transactions API** continues to validate tokens against the **old key**, while the **Auth service** started issuing tokens with the **new key**.
- Result: All transfers failed with `401 Unauthorized` error: `IDX10503: Signature validation failed`.

---

### **Additional Notes**
- **Database was not the issue** — slow queries were noted but deemed normal.
- **Support queue spiked** (40+ calls in 15 mins) — indicating high customer impact.
- **Rollback was successful** — error rate dropped below 1% within 10 minutes of rollback.
- **Post-incident review** scheduled for 2026-09-16.

--- 

✅ **Summary**: A config deploy rotated JWT keys without updating the Transactions API’s validation key, causing a widespread 401 error. Detected via alert at 09:47, resolved via rollback at 10:44. Recovery time: 55 minutes from alert to resolution.
