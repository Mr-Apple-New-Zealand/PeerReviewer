**Regression Quantification & Analysis**

**Summary:**
The statements page became unusable after release 4.7.0, with GetStatementLineQuery requests taking 35k–73kms (vs 300–3500ms before), causing timeouts and thread pool starvation. The regression is not due to a new feature but a performance degradation of an existing query.

---

**Current State:**
- **Status:** Open | Resolution: Unresolved | Priority: High
- **Assignee:** Tom Becker (initially, but comment suggests he may be wrong about scope)
- **Blocking:** No fix version or sprint assigned; no clear owner for follow-up after Tom’s “not our code” comment.

---

**Key Details:**

**Before 4.7.0 (statements-api-before.log):**
- GetStatementLineQuery: 281ms–319ms (avg ~300ms)
- GetAccountSummaryQuery: 3102ms–3255ms (avg ~3200ms)
- No long-running requests or thread starvation
- No migration applied

**After 4.7.0 (statements-api-after.log):**
- GetStatementLineQuery: 35171ms–72974ms (avg ~55kms)
- GetAccountSummaryQuery: 3188ms–3402ms (avg ~3300ms — *slightly worse, but not the main issue*)
- Thread pool starvation detected: 31 queued work items, 0 threads available for 4200ms
- Applied migration: `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines` — likely the root cause

---

**What Changed:**
- **GetStatementLineQuery** performance degraded from ~300ms to 55kms — **~180x slower**
- **Thread pool starvation** — indicates system resource exhaustion, not just query slowness
- **Migration applied** — `ReindexStatementLines` — likely caused data reindexing, which may have triggered heavy load or memory/cpu spikes
- **No change to GetAccountSummaryQuery** — it’s still slow, but not the regression trigger

---

**What Did Not Change:**
