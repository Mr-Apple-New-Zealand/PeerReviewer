**Summary:**  
A transfer retry after timeout on mobile web causes the source account to be debited twice while the destination is credited once. This results in a duplicate debit, which has affected three customers. The issue is reproducible in staging with simulated latency and a retry action.

**Current state:**  
- **Status:** In Progress  
- **Assignee:** Priya Nair  
- **Resolution:** Unresolved  
- **Priority:** High (changed from Medium on 2026-09-08)

- **Fix version:** 3.2.0  
- **Sprint:** Sprint 38 (2026-09-09 to 2026-09-22)  
- **PR:** #218 is open and awaiting code review.

**Key details:**  
- **Steps to reproduce (mobile web):**  
  1. Start a transfer between own accounts.  
  2. Introduce >30s latency (via toxiproxy).  
  3. Tap “Retry” after “Request timed out (ERR_TXN_504)”.  
- **Expected:** One debit, one credit.  
- **Actual:** Two debits, one credit.  
- **Logs show:**  
  - First debit committed (txn=8841)  
  - Credit failed (timeout)  
  - Second debit committed (txn=8842)  
  - Credit succeeded (txn=8842)  
- **Affected components:** Transactions (mobile web)  
- **Affects version:** 3.1.4  
- **Acceptance criteria:**  
  - Retry should not create duplicate debits.  
  - Debit and credit must be atomic (in one transaction).  
  - Idempotency key must be sent on retry.
