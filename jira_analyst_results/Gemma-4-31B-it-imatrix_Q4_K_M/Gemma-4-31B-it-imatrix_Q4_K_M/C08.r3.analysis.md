**Summary**
BANK-360 is the migration of transactional emails from an SMTP relay to the EmailHub API. The implementation of the client, templates, and webhook handling is complete; the ticket is now awaiting a final load test.

**Current State**
*   **Status:** In Progress
*   **Owner:** Hana Sato
*   **Blocker:** The load test is blocked until the performance environment becomes available, which is booked for the week of 2026-09-21.

**Decisions Made**
*   **Attachments:** Statements will be sent as secure download links rather than attachments. This replaces a previous plan to compress PDFs to fit under the EmailHub 10 MB limit ("EH-413 Payload Too Large"). Attachments are now considered out of scope.
*   **Fallback Strategy:** The SMTP relay will be maintained as a fallback until 2026-12-31.
*   **Deployment:** The migration will be controlled via a feature flag `EMAILHUB_ENABLED` to allow for immediate rollbacks without a new deployment.
