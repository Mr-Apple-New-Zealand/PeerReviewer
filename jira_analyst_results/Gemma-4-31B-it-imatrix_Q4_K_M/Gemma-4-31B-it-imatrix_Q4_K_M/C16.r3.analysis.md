### Analysis of Log Comparison for BANK-482

The logs show a severe performance regression specifically affecting the `GetStatementLineQuery` in version 4.7.0, coinciding with a database migration.

**Quantification of Regression**
The `GetStatementLineQuery` has slowed down by several orders of magnitude:
*   **Baseline (v4.6.3):** Average response time was approximately **300ms** (range: 281ms – 319ms).
*   **Post-Release (v4.7.0):** Response times increased to between **33,757ms (~33.7s)** and **72,974ms (~73s)**.
*   **Magnitude:** This represents a minimum increase of **~112x** and a maximum increase of **~243x** in latency.

**What Changed**
*   **`GetStatementLineQuery` Latency:** Shifted from sub-second responses to 33–73 seconds, triggering multiple `[WRN] Long-running request` entries.
*   **System Stability:** The v4.7.0 log records a `ThreadPool starvation detected` event (00:24:11) with 31 work items queued and 0 threads available for 4200ms.
*   **Database State:** Version 4.7.0 applied a migration: `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`.

**What Did Not Change**
*   **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`:** Performance remained stable at approximately **3.0s – 3.4s** in both versions.
*   **`ExportStatementCommand`:** Performance remained stable at approximately **1.8s – 1.9s**.
*   **Health Check:** The `/healthz` endpoint reported "Healthy" in both logs, with database response times remaining low (6ms vs 9ms).

**What the Logs Cannot Establish**
*   **Causality:** The logs show a correlation between the `ReindexStatementLines` migration and the performance drop, but they do not explicitly prove the migration caused the slowness.
*   **Root Cause of Starvation:** It is unclear if the `ThreadPool starvation` is a result of the slow `GetStatementLineQuery` occupying all available threads, or if a separate threading issue is causing the queries to slow down.
*   **Client-Side Experience:** While the ticket describes browser timeouts, the logs only show server-side completion times; they cannot confirm exactly when or why the browser connection is dropped.

***

**Inference on Internal Conflict**
Based on the logs, Ravi Kumar's suggestion that the `GetAccountSummaryQuery` is the cause is incorrect; that query's performance is consistent across both versions. The regression is isolated to `GetStatementLineQuery`. This contradicts Tom Becker's claim that nothing in 4.7.0 touched statements, as the logs show a statement-related migration (`ReindexStatementLines`) was applied during the 4.7.0 startup.
