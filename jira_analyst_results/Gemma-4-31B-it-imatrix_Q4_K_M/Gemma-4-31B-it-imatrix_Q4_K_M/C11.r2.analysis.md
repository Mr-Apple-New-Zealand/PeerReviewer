The customer is seeing the following error: "Error! To keep using mobile app, please log in to Online Banking and fill the customer's questionnaire."

### Summary
Users are unable to log into the mobile app (version 5.4.1) because a blocking error message appears, instructing them to complete a questionnaire via Online Banking. This has been verified as a general issue rather than a single-user problem, as it was reproduced on a test account.

### Current state
*   **Status:** Open
*   **Owner:** Unassigned
*   **Blocking progress:** No assignee has been appointed to investigate or resolve the issue.

### Key details
*   **Steps to reproduce:** Attempt to log into the mobile app with valid credentials.
*   **Affected versions:** mobile 5.4.1.
*   **Affected components:** Mobile App, Auth.
*   **Acceptance criteria:** Missing.

### Risks and gaps
*   **Ambiguity:** It is unclear if the app is intended to block login entirely for users who haven't completed the questionnaire or if this should be a non-blocking warning (as noted by Tom Becker in MOB-104).
*   **Missing information:** There are no links to the functional requirements for the "customer's questionnaire" to verify the expected behavior.

### Suggested next actions
*   **Product Owner:** Clarify whether this blocking behavior is the intended design for users who have not completed the questionnaire.
*   **Development Lead:** Assign the ticket to a developer to investigate the Auth component.
