**Summary**
BANK-360 is a request to migrate transactional emails from an SMTP relay to the EmailHub API. The implementation of the client, template migration, and webhook handling for bounces and complaints is complete; only the final load test remains.

**Current State**
*   **Status:** In Progress
*   **Owner:** Hana Sato
*   **Blocking Progress:** Availability of the performance environment, which is booked for the week of 2026-09-21.

**Key Decisions**
*   **Attachment Handling:** Statements will be sent as secure download links rather than attachments. This replaces an earlier plan to compress PDFs to meet EmailHub's 10 MB limit; attachments are now considered out of scope (decided 2026-08-05).
*   **Fallback Strategy:** The SMTP relay will be maintained as a fallback until 2026-12-31. Access to EmailHub will be controlled via the `EMAILHUB_ENABLED` feature flag to allow for a rollback without requiring a new deployment (decided 2026-08-20).

**Remaining Work**
*   **Load Testing:** Execution of a load test at 3x peak volume before cutover.

**Risks and Gaps**
*   **Stability:** The CI Bot has reported frequent integration-test failures in `EmailTemplateRenderingTests`, which are noted as "flaky" across multiple branches (e.g., `feature/emailhub-templates`, `feature/emailhub-webhooks`).
*   **Urgency:** There is significant pressure from stakeholders (Lena Fischer, Tom Becker, and Marcus Reid) due to customer feedback regarding outdated email styling.
*   **Missing Information:** No specific acceptance criteria for the load test (other than the 3x volume) are detailed in the ticket.
