**Analysis of Logs for BANK-482**

The `GetStatementLineQuery` has suffered a severe performance regression, increasing from an average of ~300ms to between 33s and 73s (a 110x to 240x increase). This has led to ThreadPool starvation on the API.

**Quantified Regression**
*   **GetStatementLineQuery:** 
    *   Before (4.6.3): ~281ms to 319ms.
    *   After (4.7.0): 33,757ms to 72,974ms.
*   **System Stability:** The "after" log records a `ThreadPool starvation detected` event (31 work items queued, 0 threads available for 4200ms), which was not present in the "before" log.

**What Changed**
*   **API Version:** Updated from 4.6.3 to 4.7.0.
*   **Database State:** A migration was applied at 2026-09-14 21:05:36: `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`.
*   **Performance:** Only `GetStatementLineQuery` shows a significant slowdown.

**What Did Not Change**
*   **GetAccountSummaryQuery:** Remained stable (Before: 2987ms–3341ms | After: 3011ms–3402ms).
*   **ExportStatementCommand:** Remained stable (Before: 1840ms | After: 1902ms).
*   **DB Health Check:** Response times remained nearly identical (Before: 6ms | After: 9ms).

**Limitations of Logs**
*   **Causality:** The logs show a correlation between the `ReindexStatementLines` migration and the performance drop, but they cannot prove the migration caused the issue.
*   **Root Cause:** The logs identify *which* query is slow, but not *why* (e.g., they do not show execution plans or lock contention).
*   **Scope of Change:** The logs show a migration was applied, contradicting Tom Becker's comment that "Nothing in 4.7.0 touched statements."

**Inferences**
*   **Root Cause:** The regression is likely linked to the `ReindexStatementLines` migration, as this is the only documented change to the statements component coinciding with the performance collapse of the `GetStatementLineQuery`.
*   **System Impact:** The browser timeouts reported by Hana Sato are likely caused by the `GetStatementLineQuery` exceeding the 10,000ms threshold and eventually triggering ThreadPool starvation.
