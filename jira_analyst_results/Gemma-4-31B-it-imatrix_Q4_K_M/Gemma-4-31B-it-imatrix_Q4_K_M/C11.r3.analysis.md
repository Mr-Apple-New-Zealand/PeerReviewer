**Summary**
Users are unable to log in to the mobile app (v5.4.1) because they are blocked by a mandatory requirement to complete a questionnaire via Online Banking. The issue is reproducible on test accounts.

**Exact Error Message**
"Error! To keep using mobile app, please log in to Online Banking and fill the customer's questionnaire."

**Current State**
* **Status:** Open
* **Owner:** Unassigned
* **Blocker:** Lack of clarity on whether the app should entirely block login or simply provide a warning.

**Key Details**
* **Steps to reproduce:** Attempt to log in to the mobile app (specifically observed with user TESTRELAXLUX4).
* **Affected components:** Mobile App, Auth.
* **Affected version:** mobile 5.4.1.
* **Acceptance Criteria:** Missing.

**Risks and Gaps**
* **Ambiguity:** It is unknown if this behavior is an intended business rule or a bug.
* **Missing Information:** There are no details on which specific questionnaire is required or why it is triggering a hard block on the mobile login flow.
* **Dependencies:** The resolution depends on the "Online Banking" platform's questionnaire status.

**Suggested Next Actions**
* **Product Owner:** Clarify if the "customer's questionnaire" should be a hard block preventing mobile app access.
* **Development Team:** Once the requirement is clarified, assign the ticket to investigate the trigger logic for this error message.
