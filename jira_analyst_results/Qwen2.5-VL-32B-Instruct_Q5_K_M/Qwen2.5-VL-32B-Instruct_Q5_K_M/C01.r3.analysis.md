### Analysis of Ticket BANK-412

#### 1. **Summary**
The issue occurs when a customer retries a timed-out transaction on the mobile web interface, resulting in the source account being debited twice while the destination account is credited only once. This problem affects customers using version 3.1.4 and has been reproduced in staging with specific latency conditions.

---

- **Assignee**: Priya Nair

#### 3. **Key Details**
- **Steps to Reproduce**:
  1. Log in on mobile web and initiate a transfer between two accounts.
  2. Introduce more than 30 seconds of latency between the API and database.
  3. When the timeout error (`ERR_TXN_504`) appears, tap Retry.
- **Affected Components**: Transactions
- **Affects Versions**: 3.1.4
- **Fix Versions**: 3.2.0 (targeted for release on 2026-09-24)
- **Acceptance Criteria**:
  - The retry mechanism should ensure idempotency, so duplicate debits do not occur.
  - Debit and credit operations must be atomic to prevent partial transactions.

### Supporting Detail

#### Description Analysis
- The issue arises due to two main problems:
  1. **Lack of Idempotency**: The Retry button submits a new request without an idempotency key, causing the server to treat it as a new transfer.
  2. **Non-Atomic Operations**: The debit and credit are not part of a single database transaction, allowing the debit to commit even if the credit fails.

#### Log Excerpt
- The logs show:
  - A debit was committed (`txn=8841`), followed by a credit failure due to timeout.
  - Another debit (`txn=8842`) was committed upon retry, while the credit succeeded this time.
