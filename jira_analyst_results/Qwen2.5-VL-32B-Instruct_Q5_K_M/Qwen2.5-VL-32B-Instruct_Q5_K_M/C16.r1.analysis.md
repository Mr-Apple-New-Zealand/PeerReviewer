### Analysis of BANK-482: Statements page unusable since release 4.7.0

#### Summary:
The statements page has become significantly slower after the release of version 4.7.0, with requests for `GetStatementLineQuery` taking over a minute to complete or timing out in the browser. This issue is impacting customer service and causing branch staff to redirect customers to call back.

### Quantitative Comparison of Logs:
#### Before Release (4.6.3):
- **`GetStatementLineQuery`:**
  - Average duration: ~300ms
  - No long-running warnings.
- **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`:**
  - Average duration: ~3200ms
  - Known to be slow but within acceptable thresholds.
- **ThreadPool:** No starvation or resource contention issues.

#### After Release (4.7.0):
- **`GetStatementLineQuery`:**
  - Average duration: ~35,000ms (over 100x slower than before).
  - Multiple long-running warnings (>10,000ms threshold).
- **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`:**
  - Average duration: ~3200ms (unchanged from before).
  - Still slow but not the primary concern.
- **ThreadPool:** Starvation detected, indicating resource contention.

#### Key Observations:
- The `GetStatementLineQuery` performance regressed dramatically after version 4.7.0, with durations increasing by over 100x.
- The `GetAccountSummaryQuery` remained slow but unchanged, supporting Ravi Kumar's hypothesis that it is a known issue but not the primary cause of the regression.
- The reindexing migration (`20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`) may have indirectly impacted query performance, though this requires further investigation.
