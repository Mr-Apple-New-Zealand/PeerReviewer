### Analysis of BANK-398: Export account statements

---

#### **1. Summary**
The ticket requests the implementation of a feature allowing customers to export their account statements in PDF and CSV formats for a chosen date range of up to 12 months. The feature is intended for customer record-keeping and sharing with accountants. However, there is a conflict regarding whether both PDF and CSV formats are required for the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) in version 3.2.0.
