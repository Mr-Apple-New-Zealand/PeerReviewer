### Analysis of Ticket BANK-412

#### 1. **Summary**
The issue occurs when a customer retries a timed-out transaction on the mobile web platform, resulting in the source account being debited twice while the destination account is credited only once. This problem arises due to a lack of idempotency handling and improper database transaction management.

#### 3. **Key Details**
- **Steps to Reproduce**:

  3. When the timeout error (`ERR_TXN_504`) appears, tap Retry.
