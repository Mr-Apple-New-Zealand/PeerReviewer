### Quantified Regression:
#### Before Release (4.6.3):
- **`GetStatementLineQuery`:** Average duration ~300ms.
- **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`:** Average duration ~3,100ms.
- No long-running requests exceeding 10,000ms.

#### After Release (4.7.0):
- **`GetStatementLineQuery`:** Average duration increased to over 35,000ms (up to 72,974ms).
- **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`:** Average duration remained relatively stable (~3,200ms).
- Multiple long-running requests exceeding 10,000ms.
- ThreadPool starvation occurred due to excessive long-running requests.

---

### Conclusion:
The logs clearly show a significant performance regression in `GetStatementLineQuery` after the release of 4.7.0. While `GetAccountSummaryQuery` remains slow but consistent, the dramatic increase in `GetStatementLineQuery` durations is the primary concern. The reindexing migration and thread pool starvation are likely contributing factors that require further investigation. Immediate action is needed to identify and mitigate the root cause of this regression.
