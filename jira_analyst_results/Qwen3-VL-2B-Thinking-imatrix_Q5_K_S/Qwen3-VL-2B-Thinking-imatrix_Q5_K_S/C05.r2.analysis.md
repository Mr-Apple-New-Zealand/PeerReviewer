### Summary  
BANK-440 and BANK-425 both report **balance updates failing after transfers** in different contexts (dashboard vs. home screen), but share the **same root cause**: caching not being invalidated after transactions. The issue involves stale balances persisting due to local storage cache mismanagement (BalanceCache), which fails to refresh after successful transfers.
