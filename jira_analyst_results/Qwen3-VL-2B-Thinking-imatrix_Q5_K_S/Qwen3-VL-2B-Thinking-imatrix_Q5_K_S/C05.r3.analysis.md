### Summary  
BANK-440 and BANK-425 both describe identical core issues: the web dashboard (home screen) fails to update balance immediately after a successful transfer, with symptoms including persistent old balance values and delayed updates. BANK-440 explicitly notes the update only occurs after logging out and back in, while BANK-425 describes a longer-term imbalance where refreshing the page does not resolve the issue.
