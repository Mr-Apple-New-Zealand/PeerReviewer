**Summary**  
A bug occurred where a customer transferred $250 from their cheque account to savings on mobile web. After a 30-second timeout ("ERR_TXN_504"), tapping "Retry" caused the source account to be debited twice ($250 each), while savings was credited only once. The root cause involves retrying the transfer without idempotency key handling, leading to two debits and one credit instead of one each.
