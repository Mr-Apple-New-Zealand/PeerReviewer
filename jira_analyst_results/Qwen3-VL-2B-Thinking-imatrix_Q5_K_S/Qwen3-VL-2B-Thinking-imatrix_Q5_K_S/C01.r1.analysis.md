### Summary  
A customer reported two debits to the source account (after a timeout) during a mobile web transfer, despite the expected single debit/credit transaction. This occurred when a 30-second latency was introduced between the API and database, triggering a retry that caused duplicate debits due to lack of idempotency and transaction isolation.
