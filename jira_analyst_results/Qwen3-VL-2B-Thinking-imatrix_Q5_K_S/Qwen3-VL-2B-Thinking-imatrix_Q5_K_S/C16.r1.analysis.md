### Summary
The Statements page is unusable since version 4.7.0, with customer statements taking over a minute or times out in the browser. The regression stems from a significant increase in the time taken by `GetStatementLineQuery` requests, which now exceeds 10 seconds (the threshold for long-running requests) compared to the baseline log from 4.6.3. The issue is consistently observed in the `GetStatementLineQuery` execution time, causing timeouts. The `GetAccountSummaryQuery` time increased marginally but remains under 10 seconds, indicating the problem is isolated to the `GetStatementLineQuery` endpoint.

### Current state
- **Status**: Open (Unresolved)  
- **Owner**: Tom Becker (assignee)  
- **Blocking factor**: Performance regression in `GetStatementLineQuery` requests; no progress on root cause investigation.  
- **Critical issue**: The page times out or stalls for >1 minute due to slow `GetStatementLineQuery` (confirmed by logs), which is the core symptom.

### Key details
- **Steps to reproduce**:  
  1. Open a customer's statement in the Statements page.  
  2. Page times out or takes >60 seconds (over 1 minute).  
- **Affected components/versions**:  
  - **Component**: Statements (API endpoint).  
  - **Versions**:  
    - **Baseline**: 4.6.3 (pre-release log: 2026-09-13).  
    - **Issue**: 4.7.0 (post-release log: 2026-09-15).  
- **Critical log evidence**:  
  - `statements-api-after.log` shows `GetStatementLineQuery` taking **33,757ms** (33.7s) and **36,388ms** (36.4s) in the `GetStatementLineQuery` request (threshold: 10,000ms).  
  - `statements-api-before.log` shows `GetStatementLineQuery` taking **<312ms** (e.g., 281ms) across multiple requests.  
- **Critical acceptance criteria**:  
  - `GetStatementLineQuery` must complete within **10,000ms** (10 seconds) to avoid timeouts.  
  - The baseline log shows 95% of requests under 10 seconds; the issue only manifests in the after-release log.
