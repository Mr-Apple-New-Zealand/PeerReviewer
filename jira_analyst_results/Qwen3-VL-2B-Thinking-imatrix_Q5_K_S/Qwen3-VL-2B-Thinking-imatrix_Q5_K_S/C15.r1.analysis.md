### Summary  
Customers are failing to receive payment receipt emails in Production, with repeated SMTP errors (535) due to authentication failures. The issue stems from incorrect key storage in the ASP.NET DataProtection-Keys directory, which is not persisted across container restarts and lacks an XML encryptor.

- **Blocking issue**: The log shows the problem is reproducible after the container restart (18:02), and the issue is confirmed to be due to key storage configuration.
