### Summary  
The issue is that customers with email addresses containing a plus sign (e.g., `alex+bank@example.com`) do not receive password reset emails, despite no error being shown to the customer. This was identified by a customer in August 2026, and the ticket was resolved by modifying the email validation regex to allow plus signs. However, testing on staging with version 3.1.4 (build 3.1.4.17) revealed the problem persists due to the email provider rejecting the recipient address.

- **Critical status note**: The ticket resolution is "Fixed" (status: Done), but the test shows the issue remains unresolved in the real-world environment.  

### Key Details  
- **Steps to reproduce**:  
  1. Send a password reset email to a customer with a plus sign in their address (e.g., `test+qa1@example.com`).  
  2. Verify the email is rejected by the email provider with "550 5.1.3 Invalid recipient address".  
  3. Check the email provider's logs for the rejection.
