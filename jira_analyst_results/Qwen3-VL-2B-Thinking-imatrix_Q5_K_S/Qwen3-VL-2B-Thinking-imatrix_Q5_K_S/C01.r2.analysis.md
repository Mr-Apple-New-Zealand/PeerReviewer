### Summary  
The transfer retry after timeout causes the source account to be debited twice (instead of once) when a customer taps the Retry button after a 30-second timeout. This results in $250 being debited twice from the cheque account and credited once to the savings account. The issue occurs due to a lack of idempotency key handling in the retry flow, leading to a new transaction initiation without transaction atomicity.

### Key details  
- **Steps to reproduce**:  
  1. Log into mobile web and initiate a transfer between two own accounts.  
  2. Introduce >30s latency between the API and database (using toxiproxy).  
  3. Tap Retry after receiving "Request timed out (ERR_TXN_504)".  
- **Affected components**: Transactions (version 3.1.4).  
- **Root cause**:  
  - The Retry button triggers a new POST `/api/transaction/transfer` request *without an idempotency key*, causing the server to treat it as a new transaction (not reusing the existing transaction ID).  
  - Debit and credit operations are not in a single database transaction, so when the credit fails due to timeout, the first debit remains committed.  
- **Critical log evidence**:  
  - `txn=8841`: Debit committed (cheque account).  
  - `txn=8841`: Credit failed (timeout after 30000ms).  
  - `txn=8842`: Debit committed (cheque account again).  
  - `txn=8842`: Credit committed (savings account).
