### Summary
A customer experienced a timeout error while transferring $250 from their cheque account to savings via mobile web. The spinner hung for about 30 seconds, resulting in two debits to the cheque account and one credit to the savings account. This issue was identified as a bug and is currently being addressed.

### Current State
- **Status:** In Progress
- **Assignee:** Priya Nair
- **Owner:** Marcus Reid
- **Blocked By:** The issue has been confirmed in staging, but not on desktop web, indicating it may be related to the mobile web interface.

### Key Details
- **Steps to Reproduce:**
  1. Log in on mobile web and start a transfer between two own accounts.
  2. Add more than 30 seconds of latency between the API and the database using toxiproxy.
  3. When ERR_TXN_504 appears, tap Retry.

- **Affected Components:**
  - Transactions
  - Affects versions: 3.1.4
  - Fix versions: 3.2.0

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
