### Summary:
The ticket **BANK-360** aims to migrate transactional emails from SMTP to the EmailHub API, including handling templates, bounce and complaint webhooks, and conducting a load test at 3x peak volume. The project has faced several challenges, notably with large attachments exceeding EmailHub's size limit and flaky integration tests.

### Current State:
- **Status:** In Progress
- **Owner:** Hana Sato
- **Blocking Issue:** Load testing is pending due to the unavailability of the performance environment until the week of 2026-09-21.

### Decisions Made:
1. **Attachments Handling:** Statements will be sent as secure download links instead of attachments, resolving the payload size issue.
2. **Fallback Mechanism:** SMTP relay will remain as a fallback until December 31, 2026, with EmailHub sending controlled by a feature flag `EMAILHUB_ENABLED`.
3. **Webhook Handling:** Bounce and complaint webhook handling has been completed and merged (PR #230).

### What is Left to Do:
- Conduct the load test at 3x peak volume once the performance environment becomes available.
- Ensure all other components, such as templates and client integration, are fully tested and stable.
