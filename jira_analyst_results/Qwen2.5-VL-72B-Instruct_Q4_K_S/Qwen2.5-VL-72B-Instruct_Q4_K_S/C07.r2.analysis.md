### Incident Timeline

- **09:31:** Errors start appearing in dashboards.
- **09:47:** Monitoring alert fires due to TransferErrorRate exceeding 20%.
- **09:52:** Sam Okafor declares the incident and notes the transfer error rate at 64%.
- **10:05:** Marcus Reid reports a spike in support queue calls.
- **10:10:** Priya Nair suggests checking the database for slow queries.
- **10:24:** Sam Okafor identifies the issue as a JWT signing key mismatch caused by OPS-81 and decides to rollback.
- **10:31:** Rollback of OPS-81 config deploy begins.
- **10:42:** Transfer error rate returns to under 1%.
- **10:44:** Rollback is confirmed complete.
- **10:50:** Sam Okafor resolves the incident and schedules a post-incident review.

### Time Calculations

- **Time to Detect:** From the start of errors at 09:31 to the monitoring alert at 09:47, which is 16 minutes.
- **Time to Recover:** From the detection (monitoring alert) at 09:47 to recovery at 10:42, which is 55 minutes.

### Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was that the JWT signing key was rotated in the configuration deploy OPS-81 without updating the key the Transactions API validates against. This mismatch led to failed signature validation and transfer errors.
