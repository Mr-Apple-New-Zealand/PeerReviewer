### Incident Timeline

- **09:31:** Errors start appearing in the dashboards.
- **09:47:** Monitoring alert fires due to TransferErrorRate exceeding 20%.
- **09:52:** Sam Okafor declares the incident and notes the transfer error rate at 64%.
- **10:05:** Marcus Reid reports a spike in support calls, with over 40 in the last 15 minutes.
- **10:10:** Priya Nair suggests a potential database issue due to slow queries on the transactions table.
- **10:24:** Sam Okafor identifies the problem as 401 errors from the Transactions API due to a mismatch in JWT signing keys following the config deploy OPS-81.
- **10:31:** Sam Okafor initiates the rollback of the OPS-81 config deploy.
- **10:42:** The transfer error rate returns to under 1% after the rollback is complete.
- **10:50:** Sam Okafor resolves the incident and schedules a post-incident review for 2026-09-16.

### Time Calculations

- **Time to Detect:** From the start of errors at 09:31 until the monitoring alert at 09:47, the time to detect is 16 minutes.
- **Time to Recover:** From the detection of the issue at 09:47 until recovery at 10:42, the time to recover is 55 minutes.

### Root Cause

The root cause was the rotation of the JWT signing key in config deploy OPS-81 without updating the Transactions API to validate against the new key. This mismatch resulted in 401 errors and transfer failures for all users.
