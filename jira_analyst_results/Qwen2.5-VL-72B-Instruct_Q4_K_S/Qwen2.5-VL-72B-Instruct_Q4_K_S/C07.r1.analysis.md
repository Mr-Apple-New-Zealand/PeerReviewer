### Incident Timeline:
- **09:31:** Errors start appearing on dashboards.
- **09:47:** Monitoring alert fires due to TransferErrorRate exceeding 20%.
- **09:52:** Sam Okafor declares the incident and notes the transfer error rate at 64%.
- **10:05:** Marcus Reid reports a spike in support calls.

- **10:24:** Sam Okafor identifies the problem as JWT signature validation failures caused by a config deploy (OPS-81) that rotated the signing key without updating the Transactions API.
- **10:31:** Sam Okafor initiates rollback of OPS-81.
- **10:42:** Transfer error rate drops back under 1% after rollback completion.
- **10:50:** Incident is resolved and root cause documented.

### Time to Detect:
- **Detection Time:** From the start of errors at 09:31 to the alert firing at 09:47, the detection time is 16 minutes.

### Time to Recover:
- **Recovery Time:** From the alert firing at 09:47 to the error rate dropping under 1% at 10:42, the recovery time is 55 minutes.

### Root Cause:
The root cause was the JWT signing key being rotated in the config deploy OPS-81 without updating the Transactions API to validate against the new key. This mismatch led to signature validation failures and transfer errors.
