### Summary
The Statements page is experiencing significant performance degradation since the release of version 4.7.0, with GetStatementLineQuery requests taking over a minute or timing out, impacting customer service.

### Current state
- **Status:** Open
- **Owner:** Tom Becker
- **Blocking progress:** Disagreement on whether the issue is related to changes in 4.7.0 or an existing performance problem with GetAccountSummaryQuery.

### Key details
- **Steps to reproduce:** Opening a customer's statement page.
- **Affected components:** Statements API, specifically GetStatementLineQuery and GetAccountSummaryQuery.
- **Acceptance criteria:** The page should load within a reasonable time (not specified), without timeouts.

### Risks and gaps
- **Ambiguities:** It is unclear whether the performance issue is new or exacerbated by 4.7.0, as Tom Becker claims no changes were made to statements in this release.
- **Missing information:** No specific acceptance criteria for load times, no details on browser timeouts, and no analysis of the impact of the ReindexStatementLines migration.
- **Dependencies:** The logs do not establish a direct link between the performance regression and any particular change in 4.7.0.

### Comparison of Logs

#### Quantified Regression
- **GetStatementLineQuery:** 
  - Before: Completed in approximately 300ms (range 281ms - 319ms).
  - After: Significantly increased, with multiple requests exceeding the threshold of 10000ms, up to 72974ms.
- **GetAccountSummaryQuery:** 
  - Before: Completed in approximately 3000ms (range 2987ms - 3341ms).
  - After: Remained relatively consistent at around 3000ms (range 3011ms - 3402ms).

#### What Changed
- **GetStatementLineQuery:** Performance drastically declined, with multiple warnings of long-running requests.
- **ThreadPool starvation:** Detected after the release, indicating a potential bottleneck in thread management.

#### What Did Not Change
- **GetAccountSummaryQuery:** Performance remained similar before and after the release, suggesting this query is not the primary cause of the new issue.

#### What Logs Cannot Establish
- The logs do not confirm whether the performance regression is directly due to changes in 4.7.0 or if it was an existing issue exacerbated by other factors.
- They do not provide information on browser timeouts or the exact impact on customer experience beyond the API response times.
- There is no analysis of the ReindexStatementLines migration's impact on system performance.
