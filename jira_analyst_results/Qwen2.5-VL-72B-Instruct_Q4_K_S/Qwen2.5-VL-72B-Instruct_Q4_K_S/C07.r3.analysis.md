### Incident Timeline

- **09:31 NZST**: Errors start appearing in dashboards.
- **09:47 NZST**: Monitoring alert fires due to TransferErrorRate exceeding 20%.
- **09:52 NZST**: Sam Okafor declares the incident and notes a transfer error rate of 64%.
- **10:05 NZST**: Marcus Reid reports a spike in support calls, with over 40 in the last 15 minutes.
- **10:10 NZST**: Priya Nair suggests a database issue due to slow queries on the transactions table.
- **10:24 NZST**: Sam Okafor identifies the problem as 401 errors from the Transactions API due to a mismatch in JWT signing keys following config deploy OPS-81.
- **10:31 NZST**: Sam Okafor initiates rollback of the OPS-81 config deploy.
- **10:42 NZST**: Transfer error rate returns to under 1% after rollback completion.
- **10:50 NZST**: Sam Okafor resolves the incident and schedules a post-incident review for 2026-09-16.

### Time Calculations

- **Time to Detect**: From the start of errors at 09:31 to the monitoring alert at 09:47, the time to detect is 16 minutes.
- **Time to Recover**: From the monitoring alert at 09:47 to the error rate returning to under 1% at 10:42, the time to recover is 55 minutes.

### Root Cause

The root cause of the incident was the rotation of the JWT signing key in config deploy OPS-81 without updating the Transactions API to validate against the new key. This mismatch led to failed transfers due to signature validation errors.
