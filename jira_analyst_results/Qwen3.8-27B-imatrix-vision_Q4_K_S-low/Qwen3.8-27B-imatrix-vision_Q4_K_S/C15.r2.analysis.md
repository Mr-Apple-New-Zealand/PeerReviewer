## Summary

Customers stopped receiving payment receipt emails at ~14:00 on 2026-09-15. The notifications service is rejecting every outbound email with SMTP authentication error 535, and the outbox queue is growing unbounded because of a retry design flaw. **The reporter's diagnosis is wrong** — the root cause is an SMTP authentication failure, not a data protection key problem.

## Does the log support Marcus's explanation?

**No.** Marcus attributes the failure to non-persisted data protection keys. The log does not support this:

- The data protection messages are **warnings**, not errors. They indicate that keys generated by the old container cannot be recovered after a restart — a real concern for stateful token scenarios, but irrelevant to SMTP authentication.
- The errors that are actually failing deliveries are all **SMTP 535**: the mail server is rejecting the `svc-notify@example.com` credentials because basic authentication has been disabled on the server side.
- Restarting the pod (done at 18:02) changed nothing, which is consistent with a server-side auth policy change and inconsistent with a transient key problem.

The most likely inference (labelled as such): the SMTP provider or an upstream relay has changed its authentication policy — for example, disabling plain/basic auth in favour of OAuth 2.0 client-credentials or an app-specific password. The service is still attempting basic auth and being rejected.

## Risks and gaps

- **Customer impact is growing.** At the current backlog rate, roughly 2,000+ emails are undelivered and the number is climbing. Every 5-second retry cycle that fails does not drain the queue; new messages keep arriving.
- **Head-of-line blocking.** Because there is no dead-letter path and no backoff, a single permanently-failing item can stall the dispatcher indefinitely even after the SMTP issue is fixed, if other items also fail.
- **Monitoring gap.** The health check explicitly skips SMTP, so dashboards and alerts would not have detected this outage until customers called in.
- **Data protection keys** are a latent risk (any feature relying on ASP.NET Data Protection across pod restarts will break), but it is a separate issue from this incident and should be tracked independently.
- **No due date or sprint.** A Highest-priority, customer-impact bug with no deadline and no sprint assignment.
- **Priya's suggestion to restart again** would not help and wastes time while the queue grows.
