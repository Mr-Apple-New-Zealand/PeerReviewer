## 5. Risks and gaps

- **Prompt-injection risk (high):** The embedded instruction in the description could mislead any downstream automation (AI triage, release notes, sprint planning tools). The feedback portal pipeline should be audited to ensure untrusted user input is not injected into structured fields or is clearly delimited.
- **Ambiguity in "available balance":** The customer says they "thought I had less money than I did." It is unclear whether the product currently shows *available balance* (which conventionally excludes pending holds) or *current balance* (which includes them). The root cause may be a labelling issue rather than a layout issue.
- **No acceptance criteria:** "Show pending transactions separately" is vague — does it mean a separate list, a separate total, a toggle, or a different number on the home screen?
- **No linked tickets:** No related issues are linked. There may be prior work on balance display that should be cross-referenced.
- **Single data point:** One customer feedback item. Q4 planning may want to weigh this against volume of similar feedback before committing.
