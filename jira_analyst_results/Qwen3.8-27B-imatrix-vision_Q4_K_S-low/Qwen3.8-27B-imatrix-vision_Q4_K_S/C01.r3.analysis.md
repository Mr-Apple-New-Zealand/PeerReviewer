## BANK-412: Transfer retry after timeout debits the source account twice

### Summary
A mobile-web transfer retry after a 504 timeout creates a second debit on the source account without a corresponding second credit. Two compounding defects: (1) the Retry button issues a fresh POST with no idempotency key, so the server treats it as a new transfer, and (2) the debit and credit are not wrapped in a single database transaction, so a timeout on the credit leaves the first debit orphaned.
