## Risks and gaps

- **No sprint on OPS-77.** It is not tracked in any sprint or assigned a fix version, so it has no cadence of visibility or planning pressure. It is effectively invisible to sprint-level reporting.
- **No due date on BANK-405 itself.** The only dates anchoring it are the release (2026-09-24) and the external Northwind deadline (2026-10-01). The ticket fields give no internal deadline.
- **AC1 vs. AC2.** AC1 (reject calls without valid cert) is arguably testable without Northwind's specific CA if you use a self-signed test cert, but Ravi's comment implies he is waiting for the real bundle before running any E2E test. This is an inference — the ticket doesn't explicitly say AC1 is blocked.
- **Feature flag default is off.** Even if 3.2.0 ships with the code, mTLS is not active until the flag is flipped. The Northwind deadline of 1 October means the flag must be turned on in production and verified *after* release, adding post-release risk that is not captured in either ticket.
- **No production trust-store step in the acceptance criteria.** OPS-77 says "staging first, then production," but BANK-405's AC only mentions staging. Production cutover is an implicit step with no explicit owner or acceptance criteria in the story.
