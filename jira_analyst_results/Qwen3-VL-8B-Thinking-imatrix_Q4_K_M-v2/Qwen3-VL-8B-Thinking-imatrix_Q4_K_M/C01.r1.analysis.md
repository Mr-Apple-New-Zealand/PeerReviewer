### Summary  
A transfer timeout on mobile web triggers a duplicate debit when users retry the transaction, resulting in two debits from the source account and one credit to the target account. The issue occurs because the retry button lacks an idempotency key (sending a new request) and the debit/credit operations are not transactionally bound.

### Key details  
- **Steps to reproduce**:  
  1. Log in via mobile web and initiate a transfer.  
  2. Add >30s latency (via toxiproxy) to cause `ERR_TXN_504`.  
  3. Tap Retry after the timeout.  
  *Confirmed in 3.1.4 staging; not reproducible on desktop web (no retry button).*  
- **Affected components**: Transactions (mobile web), version 3.1.4.  
- **Acceptance criteria**: One debit (source) and one credit (target) per transfer.
