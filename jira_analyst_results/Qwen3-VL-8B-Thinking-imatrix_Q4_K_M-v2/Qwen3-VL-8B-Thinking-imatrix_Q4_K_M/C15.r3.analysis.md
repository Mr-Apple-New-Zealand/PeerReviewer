# BANK-471 Analysis: Customers Not Receiving Payment Receipt Emails

## Summary
The actual problem is SMTP authentication failures preventing email delivery to customers, not the data protection key storage issue claimed by the reporter. The logs show repeated "Authentication unsuccessful, basic authentication is disabled" errors (5.7.139) when trying to send payment receipts. The pod restart at 18:02 did not resolve the issue, as evidenced by continued failure attempts starting at 18:02:48. The contact center report of issues starting at 14:00 conflicts with the log timestamps showing first errors at 18:02.
