### Summary  
The statements page is unusable since version 4.7.0 due to extremely slow `GetStatementLineQuery` execution, now taking 35–72 seconds (vs. 280–320ms in 4.6.3). The `GetAccountSummaryQuery` remains stable (~3,000ms), but thread pool starvation (31 queued items) coincides with the regression.  

### Current state  
- **Status**: Open, assigned to Tom Becker (no owner change).  
- **Blocking**: Unresolved due to lack of root cause analysis; the issue impacts customer-facing functionality (branch staff report customer calls).  
- **Key blocker**: The release notes explicitly mention no statement-related changes, but logs confirm a migration (20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines) was applied post-release.  

### Key details  
#### Steps to reproduce  
1. Open customer statements page in browser (v4.7.0).  
2. Observe >10s timeout or >72s loading (confirmed in logs).  

#### Affected components/versions  
- **API**: `statements-api` (4.7.0 vs. 4.6.3).  
- **Queries**:  
  - `GetStatementLineQuery`: Slows from **297ms (avg.)** to **35–72s** (log: `Long-running request GetStatementLineQuery took 72974ms`).  
  - `GetAccountSummaryQuery`: Stable (3,000–3,400ms pre/post, no regression).  
- **Environment**: Production (log timestamps UTC).  

#### Acceptance criteria  
- `GetStatementLineQuery` must complete in <10s (pre-release behavior).  
- **Missing**: No criteria for thread pool starvation (only symptoms logged).
