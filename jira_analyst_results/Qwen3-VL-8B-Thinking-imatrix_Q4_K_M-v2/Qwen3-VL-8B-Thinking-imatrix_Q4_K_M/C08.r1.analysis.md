# BANK-360: Migrate transactional email from SMTP to the EmailHub API

## Summary
This ticket aims to migrate all transactional email (password reset, statements, transfer receipts) from SMTP to EmailHub API. Key changes include implementing EmailHub client in EmailService, migrating templates, implementing bounce/complaint handling via webhooks, and conducting load tests at 3x peak volume. The team changed their approach for statements from attachments to secure download links after discovering attachments were too large for EmailHub (25 MB business statements vs. 10 MB limit), and established SMTP as a fallback until 2026-12-31 using feature flag EMAILHUB_ENABLED.
