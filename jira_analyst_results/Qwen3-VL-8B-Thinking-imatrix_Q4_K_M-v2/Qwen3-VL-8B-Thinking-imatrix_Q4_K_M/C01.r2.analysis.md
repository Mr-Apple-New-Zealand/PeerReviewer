### Summary  
A mobile web transfer timeout triggers duplicate debits due to a retry mechanism that re-submits without an idempotency key. When a transfer times out (ERR_TXN_504), retrying creates a second debit while the credit completes, resulting in two debits and one credit. Three customers reported this issue in 3.1.4, with logs confirming two separate transactions (8841 and 8842) during the failure.
