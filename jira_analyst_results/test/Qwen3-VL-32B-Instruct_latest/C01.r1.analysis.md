**Summary**  
BANK-412 is a high-priority bug where retrying a failed transfer after a timeout causes the source account to be debited twice while the destination is credited only once. This has affected three customers and requires manual financial reversals. The root cause is a lack of idempotency and transactional consistency in the transfer process.
