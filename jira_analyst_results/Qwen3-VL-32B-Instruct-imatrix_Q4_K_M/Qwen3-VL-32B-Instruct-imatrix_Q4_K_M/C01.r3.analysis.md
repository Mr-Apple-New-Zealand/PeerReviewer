**Summary**  
BANK-412 is a high-priority bug where retrying a failed transfer after a timeout causes the source account to be debited twice while the destination is credited once. This has occurred for three customers and is reproducible in staging. The root cause is a lack of idempotency and atomicity in the transfer process.
