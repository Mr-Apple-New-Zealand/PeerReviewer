## Root Cause

The config deploy **OPS-81** (09:30) rotated the JWT signing key used by the **Auth** service. Auth immediately began issuing tokens signed with the **new** key, but the **Transactions API** continued to validate inbound tokens against the **old** key. Every transfer request therefore failed signature validation, returning `401 – IDX10503: Signature validation failed`, driving the transfer error rate to 64 % and blocking all users.

Resolution was a rollback of the OPS-81 config change, restoring the old key across both services.

This 16-minute window is worth reviewing at the post-incident on 2026-09-16 – either the alert threshold or the polling interval could be tightened.

The ticket states the root cause but does not describe whether a runbook, dual-key grace period, or canary check existed. The post-incident review should cover whether OPS-81 was missing a validation gate.
