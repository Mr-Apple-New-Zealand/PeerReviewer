# Analysis: BANK-398 – Export account statements

## 1. Summary

The ticket asks for a customer-facing export of account statements (date range up to 12 months, from the Account page). The ticket is nominally "Ready for Dev" but is **effectively blocked by an unresolved scope conflict**: the reporter wants CSV-only for the 3.2.0 MVP, while compliance insists PDF with a regulatory footer is mandatory and CSV alone is unacceptable. ## 4. Risks and gaps

- **Unresolved scope conflict (critical).** The ticket cannot enter development until one of the two positions (CSV-only vs. PDF-mandatory) is formally decided and the acceptance criteria are updated. Until then the "Ready for Dev" status is inaccurate.

- **Sprint risk.** Sprint 38 ends 2026-09-22. The ticket has been in the sprint since 2026-09-09 with zero implementation progress. If the scope question is not resolved within the next few days, the 3.2.0 release target is at risk.

- **Technical risk: synchronous timeout.** Hana flags that 40k+ rows (heaviest business accounts) may exceed a synchronous generation timeout and asks whether async generation with an email/notification is needed. **No one has responded.** This is an open architectural decision that will affect the build regardless of the PDF/CSV question.

- **Compliance exposure.** If the ticket ships as CSV-only per Aroha's comment, it directly contradicts Chris Morgan's stated compliance position. If it ships with PDF, the "scope cut" rationale Aroha gave no longer holds and the PDF work (layout, regulatory footer, letterhead) must be scoped and resourced.

- **Missing CSV specification.** Even if CSV remains in scope, no format spec, column list, or encoding requirement is documented in the ticket.

- **No follow-up ticket for PDF.** Aroha says PDF "moves to a follow-up story," but no linked issue exists. If the compliance decision keeps PDF in 3.2.0, that story needs to be created or this ticket's scope must be formally expanded.
