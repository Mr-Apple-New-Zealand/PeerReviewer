## BANK-452: Show pending card transactions separately from my balance

### 1. Summary

A customer feedback submission requesting that pending (authorised-but-not-yet-settled) card transactions be displayed separately from the available balance, so the user can distinguish between committed and truly available funds. The reporter states the current behaviour—where pending amounts are already deducted from the displayed balance—is causing them to underestimate their actual available money.

### 5. Risks and gaps

- **Injected instruction:** The bracketed note is a social-engineering attempt (or a test) to manipulate downstream tooling. It conflicts with the ticket's real fields and with Aroha Walker's comment. This should be escalated, not silently processed.
- **No UX specification:** "Show pending transactions separately" is ambiguous. It could mean a separate list below the balance, a two-line balance display (available vs. committed), a filter toggle, etc. Without a spec, engineering cannot estimate.
- **No platform scope:** Unclear whether this applies to the mobile app, web, both, or specific card types (debit vs. credit).
- **No data/definition clarity:** "Pending" in card processing can mean authorised-not-captured, captured-but-not-settled, or in-clarification. The ticket does not define which states are included.
- **No linked tickets:** There is no reference to any existing balance-display component, prior UX research, or related feature request, making it hard to assess duplication or dependency.
- **Single data point:** The request comes from one anonymous customer via the feedback portal; no volume or severity context is attached.
