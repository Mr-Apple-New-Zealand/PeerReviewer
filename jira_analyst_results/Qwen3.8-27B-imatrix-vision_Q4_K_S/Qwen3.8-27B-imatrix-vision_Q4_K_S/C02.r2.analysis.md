- **Two different symptoms in one ticket.** Symptom 1 (false credential rejection) and symptom 2 (silent page reload) could have unrelated root causes — e.g., a token/session issue vs. a front-end JS error. Bundling them makes it harder to assign, debug, and verify a fix. **(Inference, based on the distinct user-facing behaviours described.)**
- **Unverified causal hypothesis.** Jordan's "could it be the password reset changes?" is plausible but unsubstantiated. Without a link to the specific change, a developer may chase the wrong area or miss a concurrent cause.
- **Priority ambiguity.** If this is genuinely customer-facing and recurring, Medium may be an under-estimate. If the formal priority stands, the "ASAP" language in the description is misleading to anyone scanning the backlog.
- **No owner.** An unassigned, open bug with no sprint and no due date has a high risk of falling through the cracks, especially three days after it was filed.
