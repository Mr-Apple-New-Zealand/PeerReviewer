# Analysis: BANK-398 – Export account statements

## 1. Summary

BANK-398 requests a customer-facing feature to export account statements for a chosen date range (up to 12 months) from the Account page, in PDF and CSV formats, targeting release 3.2.0. The ticket is currently **blocked by an unresolved scope conflict**: the reporter wants CSV-only for MVP, but compliance states a customer statement *must* be a PDF with a regulatory footer, making CSV alone non-compliant for this release. The assignee has explicitly stopped work until the conflict is settled.

## 3. Key details

**Acceptance criteria as written (description):**
- AC1: Date-range export, up to 12 months.
- AC2: PDF **and** CSV.
- AC3: Entry point on the Account page.

**Conflict with AC2:** Aroha's 2026-09-08 comment narrows scope to *CSV only* for MVP and pushes PDF to a follow-up story. Chris Morgan's 2026-09-10 comment (compliance) rejects that: a CSV alone "is not acceptable as a customer statement for the 3.2.0 release." The two positions are directly contradictory and neither has been overridden. The ticket's own AC2 still lists both formats, so the written acceptance criteria have not been updated to reflect either resolution.

**Steps to reproduce / technical specs:** None provided. No wireframes beyond the attached `.fig` file (which Lena Fischer says covers the PDF layout with bank letterhead and a regulatory footer on every page). No details on:
- What data fields appear in each statement row
- Authentication/authorization checks (e.g., can a customer only export their own accounts?)
- Rate-limiting or file-size caps
- Retention or audit logging of exports

**Missing:** Story points, due date, a concrete definition of "up to 12 months" (calendar months vs. rolling 365 days), and any non-functional requirements (file size limits, generation timeout thresholds).

## 4. Risks and gaps

- **Unresolved scope conflict (critical).** Aroha (reporter) and Chris Morgan (compliance) disagree on whether PDF is in scope for 3.2.0. Until a decision is recorded, the ticket cannot be built. The ticket's AC still says both formats, which is now ambiguous against the comments.

- **Sprint slippage risk.** Sprint 38 ends 2026-09-22. If the conflict isn't resolved this week, Hana will have ≤ 5 working days for what is a non-trivial feature (async generation, two formats, regulatory layout). Without story points the team also cannot plan sprint capacity.

- **Async generation concern (raised, unanswered).** Hana flags that 40 k+ rows for heavy business accounts may time out under synchronous generation. She asks whether async generation with an email/notification is needed. No one has responded. This is an architectural decision that affects scope, acceptance criteria, and the UI (loading states vs. "check your email" flow).

- **Compliance coupling to the PDF design.** The attached `statement_export_v2.fig` and the "regulatory footer on every PDF page" requirement (Lena, 2026-09-03) are only relevant if PDF ships in 3.2.0. If PDF is deferred, the design work may need to be re-validated in the follow-up ticket; if PDF stays, the footer must be in every page of a potentially 40 k-row PDF – a performance and layout edge case not yet addressed.

- **No linked tickets.** The description mentions a "follow-up story" for PDF, but no linked issue exists. The follow-up is not yet created or referenced.

- **No Affects version.** The ticket targets Fix 3.2.0 but lists no Affects version, making it unclear which released build the feature slots into.
