**Summary:**  
A transfer retry after a timeout (ERR_TXN_504) causes the source account to be debited twice — once for the original failed transfer and again for the retry — while the destination is credited only once. This is a critical bug affecting mobile web users, with three confirmed customer reports. The issue stems from two flaws: lack of idempotency in retry requests and separate transaction commits for debit/credit.

**Key Details:**  
- **Steps to Reproduce:**  
  1. Initiate transfer on mobile web.  
  2. Introduce >30s latency (via toxiproxy).  
  3. Timeout occurs → user taps Retry.  
  4. System creates a new transfer (no idempotency key) → second debit committed.  
  5. First debit remains committed even if credit fails.  

- **Expected:** One debit, one credit.  
- **Actual:** Two debits, one credit.
