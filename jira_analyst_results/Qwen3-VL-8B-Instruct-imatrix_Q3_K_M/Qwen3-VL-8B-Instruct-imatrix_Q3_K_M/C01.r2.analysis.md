**Summary**  
A transfer retry after a timeout (ERR_TXN_504) causes the source account to be debited twice while the destination is credited once. This occurs because the retry triggers a new transfer without an idempotency key, and the debit/credit operations are not atomic — the first debit commits before the credit times out, leaving the second debit uncancelled. Three customers affected; fix targeted for 3.2.0 release.

**Key Details**  
- **Steps to Reproduce**:  
  1. Initiate transfer on mobile web.  
  2. Introduce >30s latency (via toxiproxy).  
  3. Tap Retry after timeout.  
- **Expected**: One debit, one credit.  
- **Actual**: Two debits, one credit.  
- **Root Cause**:  
  - Retry POSTs a new transfer without idempotency key → server treats as new request.  
  - Debit/credit not in same transaction → first debit commits, second debit occurs after timeout.  
- **Affected Versions**: 3.1.4  
- **Fix Version**: 3.2.0  
- **Acceptance Criteria**:  
  - Retry must be idempotent (idempotency key).  
  - Debit and credit must be atomic (single transaction).  
  - No duplicate debits on timeout/retry.
