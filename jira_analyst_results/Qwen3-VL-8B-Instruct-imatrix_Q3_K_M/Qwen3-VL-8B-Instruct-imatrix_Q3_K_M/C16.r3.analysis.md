**Summary:**

The `statements-api` performance degraded severely after release 4.7.0, with `GetStatementLineQuery` requests taking tens of thousands of milliseconds (up to 72 seconds) and triggering “ThreadPool starvation” warnings — a stark contrast to pre-release performance where the same query averaged ~300ms. The `GetAccountSummaryQuery` also slowed slightly but remains within acceptable limits.

- **Ravi Kumar’s suggestion:** Points to the `GetAccountSummaryQuery` as a known slow query — but the logs show it’s not the primary culprit.
- **No fix versions or sprint assigned.**

**Key Details:**

✅ **What Changed (Post-4.7.0):**

- **`GetStatementLineQuery`**:
  - **Before (4.6.3)**: Avg ~300ms, max ~312ms.
  - **After (4.7.0)**: Avg ~35–40s, max ~72s — **regression of ~23,000x slower**.
  - **Log shows**: Multiple long-running request warnings, and “ThreadPool starvation” — indicating the system is overwhelmed.

- **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`**:
  - **Before**: Avg ~3,000ms (3s), max ~3,300ms.
  - **After**: Avg ~3,200ms, max ~3,400ms — **minor regression**, not the primary cause.

- **Release Note Impact**:
  - 4.7.0 included a migration: `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`.
  - This migration likely caused the regression — possibly due to:
    - Unindexed queries.
    - Data not yet fully reindexed.
    - Race conditions during migration.
    - Misconfigured thread pool or resource limits.
