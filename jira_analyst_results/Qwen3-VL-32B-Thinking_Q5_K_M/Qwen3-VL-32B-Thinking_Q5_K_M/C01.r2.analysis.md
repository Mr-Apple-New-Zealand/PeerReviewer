## Summary
BANK-412 reports a double debit issue when users tap "Retry" after a transfer timeout on mobile web. The system processes a second transfer without an idempotency key and with separate debit/credit transactions, causing the source account to be debited twice while the destination receives one credit. This has already impacted three customers with support calls on September 5, 6, and 8.
