# BANK-398: Export account statements - Analysis

## Summary
The ticket requests implementation of a customer-facing feature to export account statements, but there's a conflict between stakeholders on the required export format. Aroha Walker (product owner) wants to build only CSV for MVP, while Compliance (Chris Morgan) requires PDF with regulatory footer for the 3.2.0 release. A developer (Hana Sato) is blocked waiting for resolution of this conflict.

## Current state
- Status: Ready for Dev (but development is blocked)
- Owner: Hana Sato
- Blocking progress: Unresolved conflict between product and compliance teams regarding required export format (CSV vs PDF)

## Key details
- Acceptance criteria: AC1 (12-month date range), AC2 (PDF and CSV), AC3 (Available from Account page)
- Fix version: 3.2.0 (Sprint 38: 2026-09-09 to 2026-09-22)
- Missing: Steps to reproduce, specific technical requirements for handling 40k+ row exports
- Technical concern: Hana Sato notes "12 months of transactions for our heaviest business accounts is 40k+ rows. Generating that synchronously may time out. Do we need async generation with an email or notification when it's ready?"

## Risks and gaps
- Major conflict: Aroha Walker's scope cut ("CSV only. PDF moves to a follow-up story") directly contradicts Compliance's position ("a statement provided to customers must be a PDF with the regulatory footer. A CSV on its own is not acceptable")
- Ambiguity: The ticket has contradictory requirements with no resolution
- Missing details: No technical specifications for the export implementation or error handling
- Potential risk: The 3.2.0 release might be delayed if the format conflict isn't resolved before sprint end (2026-09-22)

## Suggested next actions
- Aroha Walker and Chris Morgan need to resolve the export format conflict before Hana Sato can proceed
- Aroha Walker should document the finalized scope and update the acceptance criteria
- Hana Sato needs clarification on handling large exports (synchronous vs asynchronous) after the format is confirmed
- The team should confirm the 40k+ row issue with performance testing before implementing the feature
