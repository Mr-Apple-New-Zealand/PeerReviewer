**Summary:**  
Two users reported that saving a secondary branch on a payment routing rule "just spins and then says it failed" with no error code. The log shows a series of transactions involving payment routing rules, with a clear pattern of failures in the **`RunRoutingRuleGeneratorCommand`** and **`BulkUpdateSecondaryBranchesCommand`** workflows. The issue is not a generic "server down" but rather a **specific failure in a Dapr service call** that is **not being properly handled** by the application, leading to a **"403 Forbidden"** response from `masterfile-api`, which is then **not properly retried or logged**.

**Key Details:**  
- **Affected Component:** Payments API (specifically `PaymentRoutingRules` workflow)  
- **Root Cause:**  
  - The **`Dapr.Client.InvocationException`** with **`403 Forbidden`** is **repeatedly thrown** during `RunRoutingRuleGeneratorCommand` and `BulkUpdateSecondaryBranchesCommand`.  
  - The **error is not a timeout or network issue**, but a **permission/authorization failure** from `masterfile-api` (the Dapr service).  
  - The **log shows multiple transactions failing with the same error**, but **no clear indication of what is wrong with the data or the system**.  
  - The **`403 Forbidden`** is **not a generic error** — it's **specific to a method call** (`branchlocation/get?ID=71&includeDetails=False`) that is **not accessible** due to **missing permissions or misconfigured access**.  

- **Critical Failure:**  
  - The **`Dapr.Client.InvocationException`** is **not being logged** properly — it's **not shown in the logs** as a **"failure"** but as **"an exception"**.  
  - The **`403 Forbidden`** error is **not being handled** by the application, and **no retry logic or fallback** is in place.  
  - The **`BulkUpdateSecondaryBranchesCommand`** fails **because of a missing `branchlocation/GetAll`** call — this is **a critical failure** that **cannot be recovered** from.  

- **What is Broken?**  
  - The **`AddPaymentRoutingRuleSecondaryCommand`** and **`BulkUpdateSecondaryBranchesCommand`** are **not properly handling** the **`403 Forbidden`** error.  
  - The **`masterfile-api`** is **not returning proper error responses** — it's **not returning a 403** but **a 404** or **no response**.  
  - The **`Dapr.Client.InvocationException`** is **not being logged** — it's **being swallowed** by the application.  
  - The **`403 Forbidden`** is **not being retried** — it's **not being handled at all**.  

- **What is Routine Noise?**  
  - **`Request GetPaymentBatchActionListQuery`** and **`Request GetReversalListQuery`** are **routine** and **do not fail**.  
  - **`Request GetStatementLineQuery`** is **long-running** (over 72 seconds) — this is **a warning**, but **not a critical failure**.  
  - **`Request SearchPaymentRoutingRuleQuery`** is **frequent** and **does not fail** — it's **not a problem**.  
  - **`Request DeletePendingPaymentCommand`** is **frequent** and **does not fail** — it's **not a problem**.
