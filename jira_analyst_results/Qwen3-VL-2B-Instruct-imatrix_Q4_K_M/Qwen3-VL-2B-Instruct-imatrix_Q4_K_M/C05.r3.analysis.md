### **1. Are any of these tickets duplicates of BANK-440?**

#### **BANK-440: Dashboard balance doesn't update after a transfer**
- **Description**: After a successful transfer, the balance on the web dashboard still shows the old value. It only updates after logging out and back in.
- **Key Issue**: The dashboard **fails to reflect the updated balance immediately** after a transfer.
- **Root Cause**: The balance is **not being updated in the UI** after the transfer, despite the API returning the correct value.
- **Caching**: The ticket mentions that the **balance is cached in local storage** (BalanceCache), which is **not invalidated** after a transfer.
- **Status**: Open, unresolved, medium priority.

#### **BANK-425: Account balance stale on home screen after moving money**
- **Description**: Customers see **out-of-date balances** on the home screen after moving money. Refreshing the page doesn’t help.
- **Key Issue**: The **web front-end cache is stale** after a transfer.
- **Caching**: The ticket notes that **BalanceCache is used and is cached for 15 minutes**, and **it is not invalidated** after a transfer.
- **Fix**: The issue is **not** about the API, but about **caching**.
- **Status**: Open, unresolved, medium priority.

#### **MOB-88: Push notification after transfer shows pre-transfer balance**
- **Description**: Push notification says "Your new balance is $X", but $X is the **pre-transfer balance**.
- **Key Issue**: The **notification is showing the old balance** before the transfer, because it’s based on **TransferRequested** event, not **TransferCompleted**.
- **Fix**: The notification service should use **TransferCompleted** event instead.
- **Status**: In Progress, unresolved, low priority.
