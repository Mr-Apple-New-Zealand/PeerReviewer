**Summary:**  
A bug in the mobile web transfer functionality where a customer's $250 transfer is being debited twice and credited only once after a timeout. The issue occurs when the transfer request times out and the user taps "Retry," leading to a duplicate debit. This is due to two main problems:  
1. The retry action does not include an idempotency key, causing the server to treat it as a new transfer.  
2. The debit and credit are not in a single database transaction, so the first debit (which is committed) remains active while the credit fails due to timeout.

  3. When the system shows "Request timed out (ERR_TXN_504)," tap Retry.  
- **Expected Behavior:** One debit and one credit.  
- **Actual Behavior:** Two debits, one credit.  
- **Affected Components:** Transactions (via `TransactionService.Transfer`)

- **Root Cause:**

  - The debit and credit are not in a single transaction, so the first debit is committed even after the credit fails.
