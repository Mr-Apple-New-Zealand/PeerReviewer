**Summary:**  
A bug in the mobile web transfer functionality where a customer's $250 transfer results in **two debits** and **one credit** due to a **retry mechanism that triggers a new transfer without idempotency**, causing the transaction to be processed twice. The issue occurs when the transfer times out and the user taps "Retry," leading to a **duplicate debit** and **partial credit**.

**Key Details:**  
- **Reproduction Steps:**  
  1. Initiate a transfer between two own accounts on mobile web.  
  2. Introduce latency (e.g., via toxiproxy) to simulate a 30-second delay.  
  3. Tap "Retry" after the "Request timed out (ERR_TXN_504)" error.  
- **Expected Behavior:** One debit and one credit.  
- **Actual Behavior:** Two debits, one credit.  
- **Root Cause:**  
  - The retry action sends a **new transfer request** without an **idempotency key**, causing the server to treat it as a **new transaction**.  
  - The **debit and credit are not in a single database transaction**, so the **first debit is committed** even though the **credit fails** due to timeout.  
- **Affected Versions:** 3.1.4 (current) → 3.2.0 (target release)  
- **Impact:** 3 customers reported this (support calls on 5, 6, and 8 September).  
- **Logs Show:**  
  - `debit committed txn=8841` → `credit failed txn=8841: timeout` → `debit committed txn=8842` → `credit committed txn=8842`  
  - This shows **two separate debits** and **one credit**, confirming the issue.
