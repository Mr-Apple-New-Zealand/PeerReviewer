**Summary:**

The ticket **BANK-360** is a story to migrate transactional email (password reset, statements, transfer receipts) from the SMTP relay to the EmailHub API. The goal is to move the EmailHub client in the EmailService, migrate templates, handle bounce/complaints via webhooks, and perform load testing at 3x peak volume before cutover.
