**Summary:**  
The Jira ticket **BANK-482** reports a regression in the **Statements page** of the banking system, where the **statement query** is now taking **over a minute** or **timing out** after the release of **4.7.0**, compared to the **pre-release behavior**. The logs show that the **`GetAccountSummaryQuery`** and **`GetStatementLineQuery`** are both slow, with **long-running requests** and **thread pool starvation** after the release. The issue is **not** in the **notifications or admin screens**, as claimed by Tom Becker.

---

**Current State:**  
- **Status:** Open | **Resolution:** Unresolved | **Priority:** High  
- **Assignee:** Tom Becker  
- **Reporter:** Hana Sato  
- **Created:** 2026-09-15  
- **Affects:** Version 4.7.0  
- **Components:** Statements  
- **Root Cause:** Performance regression in the **statements API** due to **slow query processing** and **system resource exhaustion** (thread pool starvation).  

---

**Key Details:**  
- **Logs:**  
  - `statements-api-before.log` (baseline, pre-4.7.0):  
    - `GetAccountSummaryQuery` takes **~3.1 seconds** (3102ms)  
    - `GetStatementLineQuery` takes **~300ms** (avg)  
    - No warnings or long-running requests  
    - No thread pool issues  
  - `statements-api-after.log` (post-4.7.0):  
    - `GetAccountSummaryQuery` takes **~3.4 seconds** (3402ms)  
    - `GetStatementLineQuery` takes **~35 seconds** (avg)  
    - **Multiple long-running requests** (e.g., 35,171ms, 36,545ms, 72,015ms)  
    - **ThreadPool starvation** detected  
    - **`GetStatementLineQuery`** is the **primary bottleneck**  

- **Timeframe:**  
  - **Baseline window:** 2026-09-13 00:18:00 to 00:25:00 (UTC)  
  - **Post-release window:** 2026-09-15 00:18:00 to 00:25:00 (UTC)  

- **Time of release:** 2026-09-15 (same day as the log capture)  
- **Environment:** Production
