**Summary:**  
The Jira ticket **BANK-482** reports a regression in the **Statements page** of the banking system, where the **statement query is now taking over a minute or timing out** after the release of **version 4.7.0**. The issue is reported as **High Priority** and is **Open** with **Unresolved** status. The logs provided show the **statements API** logs before and after the release, and the **key change** is in the **performance of the `GetStatementLineQuery` and `GetAccountSummaryQuery`** requests.

- **Status:** Open  
- **Resolution:** Unresolved  
- **Priority:** High  
- **Assignee:** Tom Becker

- **Affects:** Version 4.7.0  
- **Components:** Statements  
- **Root Cause:** Performance degradation in the **statements API** after 4.7.0 release, with **long-running queries** and **thread pool starvation**.

**Key Details:**

### 1. **Log Analysis**

#### **Before Release (statements-api-before.log)**
- **Timeframe:** 2026-09-13 00:18:00 to 2026-09-13 00:25:12 (UTC)
- **App:** 4.6.3
- **Performance:**
  - `GetAccountSummaryQuery`: ~3.1 seconds (3102ms)
  - `GetStatementLineQuery`: ~300–310ms
  - `ExportStatementCommand`: ~1.8 seconds (1840ms)
- **No long-running requests** detected.
- **Health check:** Healthy (6ms)

> ✅ **No long-running requests** — all queries complete within **< 10 seconds**.

---

#### **After Release (statements-api-after.log)**
- **Timeframe:** 2026-09-15 00:18:00 to 2026-09-15 00:24:51 (UTC)
- **App:** 4.7.0
- **Performance:**
  - `GetAccountSummaryQuery`: ~3.4 seconds (3402ms)
  - `GetStatementLineQuery`: **Long-running** — **> 30 seconds** (e.g., 35,171ms, 36,388ms, 72,015ms)
  - `ExportStatementCommand`: ~1.9 seconds (1902ms)
- **Health check:** Healthy (9ms)

> ❌ **Multiple long-running requests** — `GetStatementLineQuery` is **exceeding 10,000ms (10 seconds)** in **over 30% of the time**.
- **Thread Pool Starvation:** Detected at 00:24:18 — **31 work items queued, 0 threads available**.
- **Warning:** `Long-running request GetStatementLineQuery` took **> 10 seconds** — **critical**.
