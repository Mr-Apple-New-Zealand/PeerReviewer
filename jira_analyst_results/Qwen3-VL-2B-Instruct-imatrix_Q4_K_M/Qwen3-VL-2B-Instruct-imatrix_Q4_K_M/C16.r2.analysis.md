**Summary:**  
The Jira ticket BANK-482 reports a regression in the **Statements page** of the banking application, where the **statement retrieval process has become significantly slower or timed out** since the release of **4.7.0**. The issue is reported as **High Priority**, and the logs show a clear performance degradation in the `GetStatementLineQuery` and `GetAccountSummaryQuery` requests, with **long-running requests** and **thread pool starvation** in the `statements-api` container.

**Key Details:**  
1. **Logs Comparison:**  
   - `statements-api-before.log` (pre-release):  
     - `GetAccountSummaryQuery` takes **~3100 ms** (3.1 seconds)  
     - `GetStatementLineQuery` takes **~280–310 ms**  
     - All requests are within the **10000 ms (10 seconds)** threshold.  
     - No warnings or long-running requests.  
   - `statements-api-after.log` (post-release):  
     - `GetAccountSummaryQuery` takes **~3400 ms** (3.4 seconds)  
     - `GetStatementLineQuery` takes **~35,000–72,000 ms** (35–72 seconds)  
     - Multiple **long-running requests** (e.g., 35,171 ms, 36,388 ms, 72,015 ms)  
     - **ThreadPool starvation** detected: 31 work items queued, 0 threads available for 4200 ms.  

2. **Regression Quantification:**  
   - **Time to complete `GetAccountSummaryQuery`:**  
     - Before: ~3.1 seconds  
     - After: ~3.4 seconds → **+0.3 seconds** (3.1% increase)  
   - **Time to complete `GetStatementLineQuery`:**  
     - Before: ~280–310 ms  
     - After: **35,000–72,000 ms** → **>35x slower**  
   - **Total time for 10000 ms threshold:**  
     - Before: **All requests under 10s**  
     - After: **Multiple requests exceed 10s**, with **some taking over 70 seconds**  
     - **Total time for 10000 ms threshold:**  
       - Before: 0 (all under 10s)  
       - After: **4+ requests exceed 10s**, with **some taking over 70s**  
     - **Conclusion:** The **regression is severe** — **from 0 to 4+ requests exceeding 10s**.  

3. **What Changed:**  
   - **The `statements-api` service has become significantly slower** due to **long-running queries** and **thread pool starvation**.  
   - **The `GetStatementLineQuery`** is the **primary bottleneck**, with **multiple instances** taking **over 70 seconds**.  
   - **The `GetAccountSummaryQuery`** is **also slow**, but **less severe** than `GetStatementLineQuery`.  
   - **The `statements-api` service has been updated** (e.g., migration `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`), which may have **caused the performance issue**.  

4. **What Did Not Change:**  
   - The **`statements-api` service is still running** in production.  
   - The **`statements-api` service has not been restarted**.  
   - The **`statements-api` service is not failing** — it is **still healthy** (as shown by `/healthz` endpoint).  
   - The **`statements-api` service is not down** — it is **still responding**.  

5. **What the Logs Cannot Establish:**  
   - **No direct evidence** of **what caused the performance regression**.  
   - **No information** about **how the migration** (`20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines`) **affected the performance**.  
   - **No information** about **what changed in the `statements-api` code** or **what caused the thread pool starvation**.  
   - **No information** about **what the customer is doing** when they see the slow page.  
   - **No information** about **how the `GetStatementLineQuery` is being handled**.  
   - **No information** about **what the `GetAccountSummaryQuery` is doing**.  

6. **Risk and Gap:**  
   - **High risk** of **customer dissatisfaction** due to **slow statements**.  
   - **No clear cause** for the performance regression — **could be due to a migration**, **code change**, or **resource bottleneck**.  
   - **No clear resolution** — **no fix has been implemented**.  
   - **No clear dependency** — **no other ticket or component is linked** to this issue.
