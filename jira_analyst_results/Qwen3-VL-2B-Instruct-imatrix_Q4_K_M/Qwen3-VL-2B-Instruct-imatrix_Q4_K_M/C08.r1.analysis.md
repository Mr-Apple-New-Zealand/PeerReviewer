**Summary of the Current State and Decisions**

The ticket **BANK-360** is a **Story** to migrate transactional email (password reset, statements, transfer receipts) from the SMTP relay to the **EmailHub API**. It is currently **In Progress** with **Hana Sato** as the assignee and is **Unresolved**.

### Key Decisions and Status Updates

- **Scope and Ownership**: The scope has been reviewed and confirmed to be **unchanged** by the team (e.g., Aroha Walker, Dev Patel, Priya Nair). The **EmailHub client** and **templates** are the core components.
- **Critical Dependencies**: The **EmailTemplateRenderingTests** are failing in integration tests, which is a **blocking issue**. This is the **primary failure mode** that has prevented the migration from progressing.
- **Technical Changes**: 
  - The **HTTP client library** was bumped by Dependabot, but it has been **verified to still build**.
  - **Merge conflicts** were resolved in **EmailService** (e.g., Ravi Kumar, Hana Sato).
- **Decisions Made**:
  - **July 10**: The team decided to **keep sending statements as attachments** and **compress PDFs to under 10 MB**. This is **out of scope** for the story.
  - **July 13**: The team **decided to change the approach** and **send statements as secure download links**, replacing the previous plan. This **replaces the compression plan**.
  - **August 20**: The team **decided to keep the SMTP relay as a fallback** until **December 31, 2026**, and **enable the EmailHub feature flag** to switch back without a deploy.
  - **August 23**: The **bounced emails** and **complaints** webhook handling has been **done and merged** (PR #230).
  - **September 9**: **Ravi Kumar** handed over to **Hana Sato**; the **templates and client** are done. **Remaining**: **Bounce and complaint webhook handling** and **load test**.
  - **September 12**: **Hana Sato** confirmed that **bounce and complaint webhook handling** is done and merged.
  - **September 13**: **Hana Sato** confirmed that **load test** is the only thing left. It is **blocked until the performance environment is available** (booked for **2026-09-21**).
