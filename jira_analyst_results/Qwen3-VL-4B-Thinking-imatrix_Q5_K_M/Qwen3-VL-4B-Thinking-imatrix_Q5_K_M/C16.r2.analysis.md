# BANK-482: Statements page unusable since release 4.7.0

## Summary
The Statements page is unusable in version 4.7.0 as it takes over a minute to load, causing browser timeouts. This is contrary to the stable performance seen in version 4.6.3 where requests completed in under 300ms. Branch staff are reporting customers need to call back due to the extended load times.

## Current state
- **Status**: Open | **Resolution**: Unresolved | **Priority**: High
- **Owner**: Tom Becker (assigned to the ticket)
- **Blocking progress**: The statements page times out in the browser, preventing customers from viewing their statements. The issue is causing customer frustration and operational impacts.

## Key details
- **Steps to reproduce**: 
  - Open customer statement page in browser
  - Wait for load to complete (now takes >60 seconds)
  - May time out before completing
- **Affected components**: Statements API
- **Affected versions**: 4.7.0
- **Acceptance criteria**: Statement page loads within 10 seconds
- **Missing details**: 
  - No specific error message in log
  - No user-facing error screen description
  - No indication of exact cause in log
