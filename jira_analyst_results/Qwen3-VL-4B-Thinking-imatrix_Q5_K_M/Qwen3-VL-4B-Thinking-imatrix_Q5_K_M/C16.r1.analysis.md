### Summary  
Since version 4.7.0, the Statements page fails to load in production, taking over a minute or timing out in the browser. The issue is specifically caused by the `GetStatementLineQuery` request becoming extremely slow (35–73 seconds) compared to under 300ms before the release. The account summary query (`GetAccountSummaryQuery`) remains similarly slow (~3 seconds) and is not the root cause.
