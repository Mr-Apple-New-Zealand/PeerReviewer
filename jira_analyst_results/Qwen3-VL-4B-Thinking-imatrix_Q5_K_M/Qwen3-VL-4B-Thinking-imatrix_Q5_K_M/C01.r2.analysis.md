### Summary
A mobile web transfer timeout (ERR_TXN_504) causes duplicate debits from the source account when users retry, resulting in two debits and one credit instead of the expected single debit and credit. Three customers have reported this issue since September 5.

- **Blocking**: Code review of PR #218 (which addresses the issue) is pending; the ticket lacks a specific due date.  
- **Sprint**: Sprint 38 (2026-09-09 to 2026-09-22), with no deadline for resolution.  

### Key Details
- **Steps to reproduce**:  
  1. Initiate a transfer on mobile web between two accounts.  
  2. Introduce >30s latency (via toxiproxy) causing "Request timed out (ERR_TXN_504)".  
  3. Tap "Retry".  
- **Affected components/versions**:  
  - Component: Transactions.  
  - Affects version: 3.1.4 (staging environment confirmed).  
- **Expected**: One debit, one credit.  
- **Actual**: Two debits (txn=8841 and txn=8842), one credit (txn=8842).  
- **Missing**:  
  - Acceptance criteria for the fix (e.g., how duplicate debits are handled post-fix).  
  - Scope for other environments (e.g., mobile app vs. desktop).  
- **Note**: Desktop web does not exhibit the issue (no retry button; submit button disabled).
