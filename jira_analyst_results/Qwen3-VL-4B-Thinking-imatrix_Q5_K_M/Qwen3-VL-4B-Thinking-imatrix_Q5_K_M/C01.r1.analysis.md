### Summary  
Mobile web transfer retries after a timeout (ERR_TXN_504) cause the source account to be debited twice while the destination is credited once. This results in duplicate debits and partial transaction success (1 debit, 1 credit instead of 1 debit, 1 credit), affecting three customers. The root cause is the retry button resubmitting without idempotency keys and lacking transactional integrity.
