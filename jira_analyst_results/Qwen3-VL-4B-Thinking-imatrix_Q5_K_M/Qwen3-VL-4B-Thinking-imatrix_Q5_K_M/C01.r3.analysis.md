### Summary
A mobile web transfer timeout (ERR_TXN_504) causes the source account to be debited twice when the user retries, while the savings account is credited once. This has occurred in 3.1.4, with three customer reports (dates 5–8 September). The expected outcome (one debit, one credit) is not achieved due to a lack of idempotency and transactional integrity.
