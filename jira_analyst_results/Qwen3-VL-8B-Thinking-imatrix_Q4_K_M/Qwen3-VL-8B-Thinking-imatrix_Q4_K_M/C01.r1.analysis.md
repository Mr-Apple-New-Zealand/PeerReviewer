### Summary  
A transfer timeout on mobile web (ERR_TXN_504) triggers a duplicate debit when users retry. The source account is debited twice while the destination is credited once, confirmed by three customer reports. Root cause: retry requests lack idempotency keys (treated as new transfers) and debit/credit operations are not transactionally bound.

- **Owner**: Priya Nair

- **Steps to reproduce**:  
  1. Initiate transfer on mobile web.  
  2. Inject >30s latency (via toxiproxy) to trigger ERR_TXN_504.  
  3. Tap Retry after timeout.
