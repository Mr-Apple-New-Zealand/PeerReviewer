### Summary  
The Statements page is unusable in 4.7.0 due to a regression in `GetStatementLineQuery`, which now takes 35–73 seconds (vs. 0.28–0.32 seconds in 4.6.3). `GetAccountSummaryQuery` remains stable (3.0–3.4 seconds), but `GetStatementLineQuery` is now consistently exceeding the 10-second threshold, causing browser timeouts. The migration `20260914_2100_ReindexStatementLines` applied in 4.7.0 coincides with the regression.
