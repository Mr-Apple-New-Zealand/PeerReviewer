# BANK-360 Analysis

## Summary
The ticket aims to migrate all transactional emails (password resets, statements, transfer receipts) from SMTP to EmailHub API. The team has completed the EmailHub client implementation, template migration, and bounce/complaint handling, but is blocked on the load test due to scheduling constraints. Key scope changes include switching from PDF attachments to secure download links for statements after discovering EmailHub's 10 MB attachment limit.
