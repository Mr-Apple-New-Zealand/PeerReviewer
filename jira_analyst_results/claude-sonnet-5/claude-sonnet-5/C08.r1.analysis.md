## Summary
BANK-360 is a migration of transactional email (password resets, statements, transfer receipts) from SMTP to the EmailHub API, covering client integration, template migration, bounce/complaint webhook handling, and a pre-cutover load test at 3x peak volume. The work has been in progress since 2026-07-01 and has passed through three assignees; as of today only the load test remains outstanding.

## Decisions made (chronological)
1. **2026-07-10** (Priya Nair): compress statement PDFs to fit EmailHub's 10 MB attachment limit; anything still too large falls back to SMTP. This was prompted by a real failure: *"EH-413 Payload Too Large: attachments limited to 10 MB per message"* (2026-07-03), since 12-month business statements can reach 25 MB.
2. **2026-08-05** (Aroha Walker, agreed with Dev Patel and Priya Nair): **superseded decision #1** — statements will instead be sent as secure download links, not attachments. Attachments are explicitly called out as **out of scope** for this story.
3. **2026-08-20** (Dev Patel): SMTP relay stays as a fallback until **2026-12-31**; EmailHub sending will be gated behind feature flag **EMAILHUB_ENABLED** to allow rollback without a deploy.
